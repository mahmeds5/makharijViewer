How it works

Three steps, and you're teaching.

Pick a letter

Choose a makhraj from the list on the left. The tongue moves into position.

Adjust the view

Use the sliders to move the tongue or jaws, and the opacity controls to see inside.

Change the camera

Use the buttons under the 3D view to look from inside the mouth, or drag to rotate.

Please note: the model is stylized and the tongue positions are approximate. Have a qualified teacher verify each point before using it for instruction.
