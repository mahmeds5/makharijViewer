How it works

Three simple steps to explore articulation and airflow.

Pick a letter or Sifah

Select a makhraj or phonetic trait. The tongue positions itself and airflow animations activate.

Adjust view & opacity

Use sliders to fine-tune tongue elevation, jaw gap, or fade anatomical layers to see internal airflow.

Inspect from any angle

Use preset views or orbit around the 3D space to examine airflow trajectory and contact surfaces.

Please note: the model and airflow animations are stylized visual aids and approximations. Have a qualified Tajweed teacher verify each point and trait before using it for instruction.
